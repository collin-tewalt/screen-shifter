## Short description (≤ 100 characters)

Separate desktops for each monitor: switch one screen's windows while the others stay put.

## Description

Windows' virtual desktops switch every monitor at once. Screen Shifter gives each monitor its own set of desktops ("slots"), so you can flip your main screen between projects while your chat, music or reference docs stay put on the other one.

Hold Ctrl+Alt to see every monitor's slots, with the windows in each. Scroll to switch the monitor under the mouse, click a slot to jump to it, or rest on a slot to spread its windows out and pick one. Drag any window, or any preview, onto a slot to move it there, even across monitors. Close a slot with its × and its windows fold into the slot next to it.

Switches slide smoothly on the GPU, and your windows are always safe: if Screen Shifter exits, crashes, updates or is uninstalled, every hidden window comes back.

## Features

- Independent desktops (slots) per monitor, up to 8 each
- Ctrl+Alt panel showing every monitor's slots and their windows
- Ctrl+Alt+scroll, tilt, or Ctrl+Alt+←/→ to switch the monitor under the mouse
- Exposé: rest on a slot to spread its windows, with app icons; click to bring one forward
- Drag windows or previews onto any slot, on any monitor
- Close a slot (its windows fold into the next one) or ask an app to close a window from its preview
- Optional three-finger touchpad swipe to open the panel
- Smooth GPU-composited slide animations (respects Windows' animation setting)
- Hidden windows are always restored: on exit, sign-out, update, uninstall and crash
- No network access, no data collection
